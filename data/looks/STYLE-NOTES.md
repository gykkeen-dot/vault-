# Style notes (proposed, to be confirmed by the author)

Patterns seen across looks 001–008 (three user-provided videos plus five from the same creator). These are observations, not final rules.

1. **Volume plus layering.** Oversized outer layer, second layer peeking out (knit, shirt hem, scarf), slim or plain base.
2. **Tonal palette with one repeat accent.** Earth or cold-blue families; an accent (mustard, teal, rust, red) repeats in at least two places (trim, shoes, scarf).
3. **Deconstruction as the focal point.** Raw seams, patchwork, distressing, chunky knit: one statement piece, everything else plain.
4. **Wide or baggy bottoms over chunky shoes.** Hems pool or break on heavy sneakers or boots.
5. **Scarf or balaclava as a face and neck layer.** Treated as part of the outfit, not an add-on.
6. **Worn-in textures.** Knit, wool, fur trim, waxed nylon, washed denim; nothing looks new or glossy.
7. **Bag logic.** Dark backpack worn casually on one shoulder; one printed or hardware accent at most.
8. **Author tags to follow up:** LGB, Kapital, ISWN, Number (N)ine appear in hashtags or prints. Confirm which pieces.
